What a protocol's token could be worth — and whether farming it pays.

Every protocol the house rule keeps is priced by a curve fitted on the snapshot's tokenized peers, tested held-out twice. Each plan is eight costed on-chain steps, netted against an airdrop slice and the yield the capital gives up. Already-trading tokens sit in their own view; they never enter the ranking.
